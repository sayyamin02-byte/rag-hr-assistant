# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (Extract)

## Casual Leave
Every confirmed employee is entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave (CL) per calendar year.
Casual Leave is credited at 1 day per month and cannot be carried forward to the next year —
any unused CL lapses on 31 December. CL must be applied at least 1 working day in advance,
except in genuine emergencies. A maximum of 3 CL may be taken consecutively.

## Sick Leave
Confirmed employees receive 10 days of Sick Leave (SL) per calendar year, credited upfront in January.
Sick Leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered practitioner.
Unused Sick Leave may be carried forward up to a maximum accumulation of 30 days. Sick Leave cannot be
encashed.

## Earned / Privilege Leave
Earned Leave (EL) accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service and may be carried forward up to
45 days. EL requires 7 days' advance notice for planned leave. On separation, unused Earned Leave up to
45 days is encashed at the last-drawn basic salary. Probationers do not accrue Earned Leave until they
are confirmed.

## Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity Leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act,
available to female employees who have worked at least 80 days in the 12 months before the expected
delivery date. Paternity Leave is 15 days of paid leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Health Insurance
All confirmed employees are covered under a group Mediclaim policy with a sum insured of Rs. 5,00,000 per
family per year, on a floater basis covering self, spouse and up to two dependent children. Coverage begins
on the date of confirmation. Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 24 months. Claims
must be intimated to the TPA within 48 hours of hospitalisation.

## Reimbursements
Internet reimbursement is Rs. 1,000 per month against a valid bill. Work-from-home setup reimbursement is a
one-time Rs. 15,000, claimable after 6 months of service. Local travel for official work is reimbursed at
Rs. 12 per km for two-wheelers and Rs. 20 per km for four-wheelers. All reimbursement claims must be
submitted within 30 days of the expense with original bills; claims submitted later are not payable.

## Working Hours & Remote Work
Standard working hours are 9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break, between 9:30 AM and 6:30 PM.
Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month with manager approval. Core collaboration hours,
when all employees must be reachable, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
